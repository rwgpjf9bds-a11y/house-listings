Mustang Lakes PID assessment lookup

Type a street address or Collin CAD property ID to see the property's annual PID assessment installments and how much is still owed. Covers both Lakes at Mustang Ranch (PID and MIA) and the Mustang Lakes Annex (Initial and Additional Major Improvements), from each district's 2026-27 Annual Service Plan Update.

    Sources: Lakes at Mustang Ranch Public Improvement District, Annual Service Plan Update 2026-27 (Appendices C-1, D-3, E-1 through K-1); Mustang Lakes (Annex) Public Improvement District, Annual Service Plan Update 2026-27 (Appendices C-1, C-2, D-1, D-2). Figures are the 2026-27 annual installment billed by the Collin County Tax Office around Oct 1, 2026, due Jan 31, 2027.

    "Outstanding" is the remaining assessment principal. Prepaying adds a $500 lien-release fee; payoff quotes come from MuniCap, (469) 490-2800, txpid@municap.com.

    Addresses are the situs addresses on Collin CAD's 2026 records, matched to each property ID. Properties with no CAD record show the ID only.

    Lakes at Mustang Ranch: Phase 1 lots carry a PID assessment only; lots in Phases 2-9 carry their phase PID plus a share of the MIA assessment. Mustang Lakes Annex: every lot in Phases 1-2 carries both an Initial Improvements and an Additional Major Improvements assessment. Annex outstanding balances are shown rounded to whole dollars, as in its roll.
